{%- comment -%} Story tabs: a slim bar of links under the top navigation. Set them in a story's front matter. Each tab needs a title and EITHER: section: the id of a heading further down this page (scrolls to it) url: another page on this site (/scrollstories/trail/) or a full link story-tabs: - title: Intro section: introduction - title: The Trail url: /scrollstories/trail/ Or write story-tabs: auto to build one tab per "##" heading automatically. Remove the story-tabs lines to hide the bar.
